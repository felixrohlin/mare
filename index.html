Who's playing?

Grown-ups

Maré adapts per child: three right in a row moves a skill up a level, two misses moves it down. A wrong answer never just says "wrong": the crab counts it out with them, then lets them try again.

Language and voice

Players

Offline use

Installed from its own web address and added to the home screen, Maré runs with no connection: everything (pictures, sounds, progress) lives on the phone. The voice uses the phone's built-in text-to-speech; if the chosen language has no voice installed, add one in the phone's accessibility or speech settings.
