Grown-ups

Maré adapts per child: three right in a row moves a skill up a level, two misses moves it down. A wrong answer never just says "wrong": the crab counts it out with them, then lets them try again.

Language and voice

Players

Offline use

Installed from its own web address and added to the home screen, Maré runs with no connection: everything (pictures, sounds, progress) lives on the phone. The voice uses the phone's built-in text-to-speech. No sound? Turn the volume up, check the silent switch, and on iPhone download the language under Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
